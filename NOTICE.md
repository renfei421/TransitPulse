# Project provenance

TransitPulse extends the COMP90024 team repository developed at the University
of Melbourne. The original coursework included data harvesting, processing,
APIs, infrastructure and reporting contributed by the team. It is not represented
as a wholly individual project.

The personal portfolio edition maintained by **renfei421** adds and documents
engineering hardening, a completed event-window experiment, budget-controlled
external inference, reproducible analysis and verified archive/replay tooling.
See `docs/PROJECT_CASE_STUDY.zh-CN.md` and `docs/INSTRUCTOR_FEEDBACK.md` for scope.
Original in-file author acknowledgements are retained. Dependencies, model names
and vendor specifications remain attributable to their respective projects.

This publication starts from a reviewed source snapshot. Original private Git
history and team meeting records are not included. Third-party dependency and
model licenses remain governed by their upstream distributions.
